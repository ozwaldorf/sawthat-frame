Firmware for the ESP32-S3 PhotoPainter

The concerts you saw, on your wall.

An e-paper frame that shows album art for the shows you have been to, pulled from sawthat.band. It fetches your concerts, finds the artwork, dithers it for the 6-color panel and caches it on the SD card, all on the device.

How it works

Set it up once, then leave it

  • Runs on the device

    No server or app. The frame talks to SawThat and Deezer directly over WiFi.

  • Asleep between refreshes

    It wakes to show the next concert, and only syncs once it has shown them all. The rest of the time artwork comes from the SD card.

  • Settings in a text file

    CONFIG.TXT on the SD card. Press BOOT while connected to a computer to edit it as a USB drive.

Artwork

Rendered on the frame

Album art is resized, color adjusted and dithered to the Spectra 6 palette, with the band, date and venue set underneath.

Install

Flash from your browser

  1. 01

    Requirements

    • The Waveshare ESP32-S3-PhotoPainter
    • An SD card up to 32 GB (4 GB recommended)
    • A USB-C data cable
    • A Chromium-based web browser
  2. 02

    Prepare the SD card

    Cards up to 32 GB come formatted as FAT32 and work as is. Larger cards come as exFAT and need reformatting as FAT32. The frame keeps its settings and the artwork it downloads on the card.

  3. 03

    Connect the frame

    Insert the SD card and plug the frame into this computer over USB-C.

  4. 04

    Flash the firmware

    Written to CONFIG.TXT on the SD card when the frame first starts.

    Choose the frame's serial port when asked, usually USB JTAG/serial debug unit. If it doesn't show up, you may need to press a button on the frame.
